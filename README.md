# 35mm-Ball-bearing
Ball bearing for 35mm shaft
PC207

    Design step
I divided my design into Two parts which are:
i) design of ball bearing parts.
ii) Assembly of all the bearing parts

i). Design of ball bearing parts
1. I first made a sketch, this sketch provide easy route for my project especially during assembly.
2. I created another body, where i designed the outer ring with accurate dimension
3. I created another body, where i designed the inner ring with accurate dimension
4. I created another body, where i designed the ball cage with accurate dimension
5. I created another body, where i designed the ball with accurate dimension

ii) Assembly of ball bearing parts
I use A2plus workbench to assemble all the parts.

Note: All dimension and design were based on research from trader and metal and fabrication shop. 
      The dimension will be stated in the description below.

      DESCRIPTION

     d. total height= 20mm
